Overview

Arni had no web presence, no way for potential clients to find him, learn about his services, or book a consultation. The goal was to build a clean, minimal site from scratch that represents his brand, communicates his offer, and converts visitors into leads through a free online consultation booking system.

The Challenge

Without a website, Arnes had no digital channel to reach new clients. Word of mouth had its limits and there was no way to scale his client acquisition. He needed a professional online presence that would work as a conversion tool, not just a business card.

Before the site, everything relied on referrals and social media. Potential clients had no central place to learn about his services, understand his approach, or take the next step on their own terms. Every new client required a personal touchpoint, which meant growth was entirely dependent on Arni's direct effort and existing network.

My role

I handled both design and development on this project, working independently alongside Arni's copywriter.

I designed the full site in Figma, developed it in Webflow, and integrated Mailchimp for email capture and Cal.com for online consultation bookings connected to Google Meet. The design direction was intentionally minimal, clean and visually strong without being aggressive, letting Arni's personality and offer take center stage.

Technical Approach

The site was built in Webflow with a clean, maintainable codebase using Finsweet attributes and custom code where needed. The booking flow was integrated through Cal.com, routing confirmed consultations directly to Google Meet. Mailchimp handles email capture and follow-up automation.

This project is a good example of how a well-designed, focused website can be a direct revenue driver. Arni did not need a complex system. He needed a clear offer, a frictionless booking flow, and a design that builds trust instantly. That is exactly what we built.
